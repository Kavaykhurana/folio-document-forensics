# Folio product demo narration

1. Folio helps reviewers compare related documents and follow every signal back to its source.
2. The demo uses three fictional PDFs. Folio extracts supported fields, records hashes, and compares the values.
3. Two declarations of annual income differ, so the app raises a review signal with linked evidence.
4. Select the income proof to inspect extracted lines alongside the original PDF page.
5. Export the dossier with findings, hashes, evidence references, and limitations in one reviewable file.
6. These explainable checks are prompts for human review; they do not determine authenticity or fraud.
