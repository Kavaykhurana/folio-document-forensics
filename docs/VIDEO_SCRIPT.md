# Human voiceover script

Read the six paragraphs in one take at a natural conversational pace. Do not say the numbers. Pause for about one second between paragraphs. The video will be timed to your recording.

1. Folio brings related records into a single review workspace, with each finding connected to its source.
2. This case uses three fictional PDFs. Folio extracts supported fields, records document hashes, and compares matching values.
3. The application lists annual income of 1.2 million rupees. The income proof lists 2.1 million. Folio flags the difference for review.
4. That signal links to evidence lines beside the original PDF, so a reviewer can check the source in context.
5. A reviewer can export a dossier with the findings, evidence references, hashes, and system limitations.
6. These explainable signals are prompts for review, not a fraud verdict. A person must verify important information with authoritative sources.
